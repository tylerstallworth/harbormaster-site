Support

Questions, problems or ideas: email tyler.stallworth@gmail.com. Please include your macOS version, the Harbormaster version (Settings → Data) and, for a connection problem, the text under Details on the banner.

Requirements: macOS 12 or later, on Apple silicon or Intel. To connect to a real cluster you need a kubeconfig (the file kubectl uses). For Amazon EKS you also need the AWS CLI, for example brew install awscli. Without either, Harbormaster opens its built-in demo cluster so you can look around and take the lessons.

Common questions

How do I connect my cluster?

Harbormaster reads ~/.kube/config automatically. You can also use + Add clusters to import another kubeconfig file, or to find your EKS clusters with an AWS profile and region. Switch clusters with the name at the top left.

My EKS cluster says "AWS sign-in needed".

Your AWS SSO session has expired, which is routine after a restart or a long break. Click Sign in with AWS SSO on the banner, approve the sign-in in your browser, and Harbormaster reconnects by itself. If it says the identity has no access, your IAM role needs an access entry (or an aws-auth mapping) on the cluster.

It says it can't run my sign-in command.

A kubeconfig can name any program to run for sign-in. Harbormaster runs the well-known ones (aws, aws-iam-authenticator, kubelogin, gke-gcloud-auth-plugin, az) and asks before running anything else. In the Mac App Store edition, sign-in tools can read ~/.kube and use ~/.aws but nothing else in your home folder, so a plugin that keeps its cache elsewhere may fail. Importing a kubeconfig that signs in another way works around it.

Does Harbormaster change anything in my cluster?

No. It only sends read requests (GET, list and watch), the same as kubectl get. The in-app Guide (What it reads, and permissions) lists exactly what it reads.

Where is there a terminal?

The Mac App Store edition doesn't include a terminal, because Apple's sandbox keeps shells away from your files and tools. Everything else, including the map, findings, Access, WatchTower and the lessons, is the same.

How do I reset it?

Settings → Data has Reset view choices and Restore default settings. Removing the app from Applications removes it; its data lives in ~/Library/Containers/dev.harbormaster.app.
